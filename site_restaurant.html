Our Story

Cooking with fire,
since always

Ember & Oak was born from a simple belief — that fire is the oldest and most honest way to cook. Every dish on our menu is touched by flame, whether slow-roasted in our stone oven or seared over open hardwood coals.

We source our produce within 60 miles of our kitchen, working directly with farmers, fishers, and foragers who share our obsession with quality.
